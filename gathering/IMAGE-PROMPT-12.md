# Gathering 12 image mark

Built-in imagegen mode produced a transparent, flat mineral-pigment motif. The source wall was inspected first; the motif was then blended only inside `(75,630,230,192)` at 96% opacity, preserving all pixels outside that rectangle. The selected motif has no hands or writing. The whole finished wall and its JPEG are the only saved pictures in the workspace.

Prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a small cave-painting pigment mark to be composited onto an existing warm cave wall. Make a compact, flat hand-painted mark: four short irregular charcoal trails converge at one small ochre pebble, with each trail ending in a simple dot of red ochre, yellow ochre, chalk white, or charcoal. Rough burnt-stick lines and thumb-smudged mineral pigment, worn uneven edges, transparent background. Keep the motif centered with ample transparent margin. No frog, no hands, no handprints, no scene, no realistic rock background. No letters, numbers, writing, captions, symbols resembling text, logos, signatures, watermarks, borders, or frames.

The first blend at 150×125 pixels was too faint on the dark rock, so I used a 230×192-pixel confined blend for the final wall. The local review and format check are in `checks/round-12/wall-review.json`; this is not independent artistic certification.
